Controls

Sound is on by default and starts on your first click or keypress. Use SOUND ON / OFF at the top to mute or unmute. The selection screen, Square, Street and Hospital each have their own music.

Win two rounds. Reduce your opponent’s health to zero, or have more health when time runs out.

Player 1: A / D move, W or Space jump, S crouch/guard. J punch, U kick, I weapon attack, K special I, L special II.

Player 2: Arrow keys move, jump and crouch/guard. 1 punch, 2 kick, 3 weapon, 4 / 5 specials (top row or numpad).

Gamepads: Left stick / D-pad move, A jump, X punch, B kick, Y weapon attack, RB special I, RT special II, LB crouch/guard. First connected pad controls P1, second controls P2.

Low and aerial attacks: Hold crouch and press punch or kick for a low attack. Press punch or kick during a jump for an aerial attack. Attacking leaves you unable to guard until recovery; landing ends an aerial attack.

The special bar starts empty. Evade a nearby punch, kick or weapon attack to gain 20 charge; completely avoid a special cast to gain 30. Special I costs 26 and Special II costs 42. Waiting and blocking do not charge the bar. Hold away from the opponent to stand-block; S / Down crouches and guards. Normal attacks do no damage on block. Direction + jump crosses over opponents with a fixed arc; you turn after landing. Get close for melee; jump over projectiles. Escape pauses. R rematches after the fight.

For local play, some keyboards limit simultaneous keys. Two gamepads avoid this. Touch controls are also available.
